Initial generation prompt
Create a new animated campfire game prop sprite sheet using the supplied images
only as visual references for this project's native art style, pixel scale,
palette discipline, material language, and top-down RPG viewing angle.

Create exactly four equal invisible cells in a 2x2 grid, read left-to-right
across the top row and then the bottom row. The same compact roadside
checkpoint appears at the same scale and position in all frames: a low ring of
charcoal-gray stones, two crossed burnt logs, and a small upright flame.
Compose for clear 64x64 use with crisp hard pixel edges, a limited muted
palette, sparse dithering, and a near-black deep-brown outline. The flame uses
dark red-orange, ember orange, restrained warm gold, and only a tiny pale-yellow
hot center. Do not use pure white, neon colors, painterly texture, antialiasing,
soft bloom, smoke, scenery, cast-light circles, UI, text, or labels.

Animate only the flame: compact left lean, taller center, compact right lean,
and a settling frame close to frame one. Keep the stone ring, logs, shadow,
scale, centerline, and bottom anchor stationary. Keep the whole prop within the
central safe area with generous margin and nothing crossing a cell edge.
Background is 100% solid flat #FF00FF with no gradient, texture, transparency,
grid, borders, or guide marks.
